# Dexplorer

## Features

- **Encounter heatmap:** pick a Pokémon and see where to find it on the map, from blue (lowest encounter rate) to green (highest).
- **Encounter details:** hover over a highlighted area to see how to catch it (walking, surfing, fishing, gift or static), the level range and the encounter rate.
- **Game switcher:** switch between Red, Blue and Yellow (Generation I), Gold, Silver and Crystal (Generation II), or Ruby, Sapphire and Emerald (Generation III, Hoenn) or FireRed and LeafGreen (Generation III, Kanto) to see each game's own encounters, including version exclusives and time-of-day differences.
- **Generation selector:** the game list follows the generation you choose.
- **Region selector:** generations with more than one region (Generation III: Hoenn for Ruby, Sapphire and Emerald, Kanto for FireRed and LeafGreen) get a region dropdown under the generation selector, and the game list follows the region. It stays hidden for single-region generations.
- **Gifts, statics, trades and roaming:** Hoenn includes starters, legendaries, Devon Scope Kecleon, NPC trades and the roaming Latias/Latios. Event-only Pokémon and places with no spot on the map (for example Birth Island and Navel Rock) are mentioned in text, without a map location.
- **Pokémon picker:** browse Generation I (151), Generation II (251) and Generation III (386) Pokémon, each with sprites from its own generation's games, and search by name or number.
- **Comfortable to use:** light and dark mode, smooth animations, and full keyboard support.

# DexCheck

**Your living dex, built from your save files.**

Add your Pokémon saves and DexCheck shows what you've caught, what's missing, and where to find the rest. It runs in your browser. Your saves are read on your device and never uploaded.

## Add all your saves at once
- One **Add Save Files** button, or drop files anywhere on the page.
- Mix games and generations in a single dex.
- Reads Red, Blue, Yellow, Gold, Silver, Crystal, Ruby, Sapphire, Emerald, FireRed, LeafGreen.
    [upcoming: Diamond, Pearl, Platinum, HeartGold, SoulSilver, Black, White, Black 2, White 2, X, Y, Omega Ruby, Alpha Sapphire, Sun, Moon, Ultra Sun, Ultra Moon, Sword, Shield, Scarlet and Violet.]
- Your saves stay loaded after a refresh.

## A living dex you can read at a glance
- A grid of all 386 Pokémon from Generations I to III.
- Live progress count and bar.
- Sort by **National Dex**, **Region** or **Generation**.
- Search by name or number, and switch between **All**, **Caught** and **Missing**.
- Filter by game to see what you've caught in each one.

## Every copy you've caught
- Each copy shows its nickname, level and where you caught it.
- Click a copy for its full stat page.

## Full stat page
- Types, gender, nature, held item and ability.
- National and regional dex numbers.
- A stats chart 
- A type matchup chart: weak to, resists and immune to, for that game's generation.
- Its four moves with type, power and accuracy as in that game.
- Moveset: level-up moves, egg moves and TM/HM/tutor moves.

## Find the ones you're missing
- Click a missing Pokémon to see where to find it.
- Every game it appears in, with areas grouped together.
- Method, level range and odds, including time of day.
- Click an area to open it in Dexplorer, with that game and Pokémon selected.

## Accurate by design
- Save reading follows how PKHeX reads saves.
- Base stats, types, learnsets and type charts are cross-checked against the original game data.
